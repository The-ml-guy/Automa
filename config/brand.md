## 4. Voice, Tone & Writing Style
- **Tone**: Direct, intellectually honest, calm, analytical, and professional.
- **Style Rules**:
  - Lead with the core insight or problem in the first two sentences.
  - Explain *why* something works or fails, not just *that* it does.
  - Use clear, plain technical language. Avoid buzzwords and inflated metaphors.
  - Write in concise, readable paragraphs.
- **Emoji Usage**: Strict limit of 0 to 2 emojis per post. Never use emojis as bullet points or decoration.
- **Hashtag Usage**: Minimal and relevant only (LinkedIn: 2–3 max; X: 0–2 max; Instagram: 3–5 max).
